# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email yure@mkzk.dev. Please do not open a public issue for a vulnerability.

Include the hub version (About shows it), the Android or Windows version, and the steps to reproduce. If the problem involves another app that talks to the hub, say which one.

## What is in scope

What the hub does and does not protect against is written down in [docs/PROTOCOL.md](docs/PROTOCOL.md#security-model). The pairing and encryption between devices is described in [docs/NETWORK.md](docs/NETWORK.md#pairing-and-identity). Reports about the limits listed in PROTOCOL.md as "not guaranteed" are still welcome, but they describe known behavior rather than new bugs.
